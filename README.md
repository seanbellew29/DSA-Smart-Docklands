Smart Bubble Barrier Project For the Dublin Smart Docklands.

This project is aimed to fight against plastic pollution. 
The main purpose of the smart bubble barrier is to clean the water and prevent plastic waste from the river Liffey and it eventually flowing into the Irish Sea. 
Plastic pollution poses critical challenges to water ecosystems across the world. Urban water systems such as the Liffey especially are estimated to be the main contributors to river plastic pollution, 
with bubble barriers plastic waste can be prevented from entering the sea dangering wildlife.

How the Smart Bubble Barrier will work.
Plastic bottles, cans etc that flow down the river liffey would be guided by the bubbles into a sort of catchment system. The catchment system/holder will hold the plastic waste until its removed. The bubble barrier will be made from an electric compressor that will generate the compressed air and the barrier would be be placed diagonalally across the river, collecting bigger amounts of debris. 
An advantage of this system is that boats will be allowed pass through without any interruptions.

In relation to my project, I will create a monitoring tool to evaluate the data of the plastic pollution types, levels and categories of waste for the Dublin Docklands project
